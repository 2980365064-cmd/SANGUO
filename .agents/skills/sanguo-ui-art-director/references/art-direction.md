# SANGUO art-direction reference

The player should feel they are reading a preserved Three Kingdoms record, not operating a polished website. Learn historical-simulation atmosphere, information order, archival reading, and ritual from references; never clone another game's assets, pattern, or layout.

Material is structural, not cosmetic. Paper layers, ink dividers, seals, wood/bamboo details, and controlled whitespace must reinforce the reading sequence. A surface can remain light and restrained; it must not become a dark HUD.

For dialogues, show identifiable participants and document-like correspondence or minutes. Replies must not read as generic chat text.
